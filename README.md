# nvda_stock_analysis

Let's take a look at NVIDIA's stock from 2021 to 2025.

## What it does
- Downloads 5 years of daily stock data from Yahoo Finance
- Cleans and formats the data, and adds a 100-day moving average
- Saves the data to a CSV file
- Charts the closing price, the moving average, and trading volume

## Tools used
Python, pandas, yfinance, matplotlib, Google Colab

## Key findings
NVDA stayed under about $30 through 2022, then rose sharply starting in 2023,
reaching over $200 in late 2025. The moving average shows downturns in 2022 and early 2025.
